# Integrated Mini-ITX Enclosure

Parametric compact enclosure with:

- Final outer body envelope 160 × 260 × 252 mm. The motherboard side (+X) is extended outward by 8 mm from the previously tested 152 mm-wide print while the GPU-side enclosure wall, motherboard, rear-I/O and SFX datums remain fixed. The GPU vertical datum is set by aligning the lower edge of its rear dual-slot opening with the lower edge of the Mini-ITX rear-I/O opening.
- Rounded outer shell assembled from separate FDM-printable base, top cap, front, rear, left, and right panels.
- Mini-ITX motherboard reference envelope and standard mounting pattern.
- Physical fit evidence supersedes the simplified cooler reference gap: the installed cooler, including the CPU/socket stack height omitted by the earlier CAD estimate, sits about 4 mm beyond the former panel inner plane. The design requirement is therefore an 8 mm one-sided motherboard-side extension, not a minimum 8 mm final clearance.
- SFX PSU reference and rear mounting provision.
- User GPU envelope 241 × 132.5 × 41 mm plus 8.8 mm rear L-ear extension (249.8 mm total CAD envelope), installed as a dual-slot card.
- Rear PCIe opening Z=84.99..190.99 mm (106 mm high) with removable raised dual-slot retention bracket. Its lower edge is aligned to the Mini-ITX rear-I/O chassis opening lower edge at Z=84.99 mm.
- The GPU cooler/shroud bottom remains 7.5 mm below the active PCIe opening lower edge and is at Z=77.49 mm. The metal rear bracket remains aligned to the PCIe opening at Z=84.99..190.99 mm.
- GPU slot/opening center X=-43 mm. The two GPU-ear M3 retention axes keep the standard 20.32 mm pitch. Their rear-view correction remains 3.6 mm to the right (+X), while the previous 3.6 mm outward correction has been pulled inward by 1.5 mm to a final 2.1 mm outward/rearward (-Y), giving axes X=-49.56/-29.24 mm and Y=-135.1 mm.
- The PCIe bracket follows the aligned opening and keeps a 1.0 mm vertical gap above the GPU bracket ear. Its horizontal shelf is 4.2 mm thick, with 3.2 mm minimum M3 self-forming engagement generated by printed-screw-joint-v1. The shelf extends outward by 2.1 mm so the holes remain closed and retain 5.0 mm rear edge land; the two bracket-to-rear-panel screws remain unchanged.
- Front panel with two 80 mm fan positions and an integrated single-plate GPU nose support. The 41 mm-wide, 31.2 mm-deep, 3 mm-thick support plane follows the GPU bottom at Z=77.49 mm, has no lateral retaining walls, and is reinforced underneath by three 3 mm-thick, 14 mm-high ribs connected to the front panel.
- The front GPU support follows the card datum, retains its continuous bearing floor and clearance from the fixed 80 mm fan frames. The former modeled 8-pin location proxy was inaccurate and has been removed; per the user's physical confirmation, the actual connector does not interfere with this cradle and the cable solution is user-owned.
- Front-panel power-button location remains X=+50 mm, Z=227 mm. The user-confirmed purchased-button variant is Φ8 mm and the target printed aperture is Ø8.2 mm; the received threaded body must be measured before production printing.
- Base-front standard 120 × 120 × 25 mm fan position centered at X=0, Y=58 mm with 105 mm square mounting pitch, Ø4.4 mm mounting holes, and rounded-slot grille.
- Generic standard-size 120 mm fan assembly reference placed on the inside of the base at X=0, Y=58, Z=3..28 mm for fit and airflow comparison. It is a dimensional reference, not a vendor-specific or printable fan.
- The base fan clears modeled motherboard, GPU, SFX PSU, front panel, rear panel, GPU support, and the lower front 80 mm fan frame. Current nominal bottom ground gap is about 4 mm and remains the primary intake restriction; fan wire exit, filter, pads, and screw heads must be checked after a real fan model is selected.
- Rounded-slot fan grilles on the front and base.
- Eight 10 × 5 × 2 mm rectangular magnets in compact adhesive pockets: four on the front panel and four on the rear panel. Final pocket size is 10.5 × 5.5 × 2.0 mm: the 0.5 mm total installation allowance applies only to the 10 × 5 mm planar dimensions, while the depth stays at the exact 2.0 mm magnet thickness so a magnet bonded against the floor finishes flush with the opening. There are no magnet-retention screws.
- Each front/rear station uses one simple local rectangular carrier only. Oversized perimeter frames, laminated backing guards and hidden connection necks are removed. Matching side-panel steel-strike recesses remain compact and are cut from the inner face without altering the exterior panel skins.
- The motherboard rear I/O opening is re-cut after panel reinforcement and has one continuous 1.0 mm clip land rather than nested reinforcement layers.
- The front and rear panels use eight single 12 × 12 × 10 mm top/bottom corner blocks, rebuilt once on the final skin surfaces while retaining the existing M3 screw-joint axes.
- The four top exterior corner joints use kit-generated M3 × 10 conical countersunk screws. At the bottom, four independent Ø16 × 4 mm printed feet sit beneath the separate base. One kit-generated M3 × 12 conical countersunk screw at each corner passes through the foot and base and threads into the matching lower block of the front or rear panel. The user-measured maximum countersunk-head diameter is 5.8 mm. The four top seats remain at their previous 0.20 mm kit-cut offset and approximately Ø6.12 mm entrance. Based on the user's physical insertion feedback, only the four foot-side exterior entries use a 0.45 mm kit-cut offset; committed build 519 measures all four at Ø6.71705 mm, providing approximately 0.917 mm total diametral clearance and 0.459 mm per side. The foot seats remain true conical geometry and are recessed an additional 1.2 mm, adding 1.2 mm usable reach while clamping all three printed parts with one fastener. At the rounded underside, the Ø13.6 mm outer land leaves approximately 3.44 mm radial material around the enlarged entry.
- Bottom-front motherboard fastening point intentionally removed.

All enclosure parts retain user-editable parameters for overall dimensions, panel thickness, ventilation, fan features, magnet clearances, foot dimensions, edge radius, screw-seat recess, measured-head cut allowance, and fastening geometry. Purchased-component standards and externally fixed dimensions remain named constants or reference-model geometry.
